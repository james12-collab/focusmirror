2. Description of Service

FocusMirror is an AI-powered study performance tracking application that uses your device's front-facing camera to measure cognitive focus indicators including:

  • Blink rate as an indicator of eye fatigue
  • Postural alignment as an indicator of physical engagement
  • Application switching frequency as an indicator of distraction
  • Derived focus scores calculated from the above indicators

The Service provides personalized study recommendations, burnout prediction, session analytics, study pattern insights, leaderboards, achievement systems, and educational tools designed to help students optimize their study sessions.

⚠ FocusMirror is a productivity tool, not a medical device. Focus scores are approximations based on publicly documented behavioral indicators. They do not constitute medical, psychological, or clinical assessments.
