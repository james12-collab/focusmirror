Acceptable Use Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective June 21 2026

Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the rules governing your use of FocusMirror. It exists to protect all users, maintain the integrity of the Service, and ensure FocusMirror remains a safe and productive environment for students worldwide.

This AUP is incorporated into our Terms of Service. Violations may result in immediate account suspension or termination.

Permitted Uses

✅ Using FocusMirror to track your own personal study sessions
✅ Sharing your own focus scores, achievements, and report cards
✅ Teachers using the Teacher Dashboard to monitor enrolled students who have consented
✅ Parents using the Parent Dashboard to view their own child's data
✅ Using Study Buddy Mode with a friend who has knowingly joined your room
✅ Using the app on shared devices where each person logs into their own account
✅ Sharing FocusMirror links with friends, classmates, and study groups
✅ Embedding FocusMirror links in school websites or educational resources

Prohibited Uses

Privacy Violations

❌ Monitoring or tracking any person's face, posture, or behavior without their explicit consent
❌ Sharing another person's focus data, scores, or session information without their consent
❌ Using teacher or parent dashboard features to surveil students or children beyond educational oversight
❌ Attempting to access another user's account or session data

Technical Abuse

❌ Attempting to manipulate, spoof, or artificially inflate focus scores
❌ Using automated tools, bots, or scripts to interact with the Service
❌ Attempting to reverse engineer, decompile, or extract proprietary algorithms
❌ Performing load testing, penetration testing, or security scanning without written permission
❌ Attempting to circumvent the midnight lock or other safety features

Account Abuse

❌ Creating multiple accounts to manipulate leaderboards or bypass restrictions
❌ Sharing account credentials with others
❌ Impersonating another person, student, teacher, or FocusMirror staff
❌ Creating accounts for minors under 13 years of age

Commercial Abuse

❌ Reselling, sublicensing, or commercializing access to FocusMirror without written permission
❌ Using FocusMirror data to build competing products
❌ Scraping or bulk-downloading data from the Service

Harmful Conduct

❌ Using Study Buddy or Class features to bully, harass, or harm other users
❌ Transmitting malware, viruses, or harmful code through any Service feature
❌ Using FocusMirror to facilitate academic dishonesty or cheating

Special Rules for Educational Use

Teachers

Teachers using the Teacher Dashboard must:

  • Obtain informed student consent before monitoring sessions
  • Use class data solely for legitimate educational oversight
  • Not share identifiable student data with parents without proper consent under applicable student privacy laws (FERPA in USA, DPDP Act in India)
  • Not use focus scores as the sole basis for grading, disciplinary action, or academic assessment

Schools and Institutions

Schools using FocusMirror under a School License must execute our Data Processing Agreement before deploying to students. Contact us for institutional licensing.

Reporting Violations

If you believe another user is violating this AUP please report it through the app or contact us directly. We investigate all reports and take appropriate action. We protect reporter confidentiality.

Enforcement

We reserve the right to investigate suspected violations. Depending on severity, enforcement actions may include:

  • Warning notification
  • Temporary account suspension
  • Permanent account termination
  • Legal action where warranted
