Account Deletion

When you delete your account:

ActionTimeline
Account access immediately revokedImmediate
Session data marked for deletionImmediate
Data permanently deleted from FirebaseWithin 30 days
Anonymized global session dataNot deleted (no personal identifiers)
Backup systems purgedWithin 90 days

Anonymized data in the global all_sessions collection contains no usernames, names, or personal identifiers and therefore cannot be attributed to you after account deletion.

Biometric Data — Special Rules

FocusMirror processes biometric indicators (blink rate derived from facial landmark analysis, postural data derived from facial position). Under Illinois BIPA and similar laws, biometric data has enhanced protection.

We commit to:

CommitmentStatus
No storage of raw biometric data or camera footage✅ Never stored
No sale of biometric data✅ Never sold
No transfer of biometric data to third parties✅ Never transferred
Destruction within 3 years of collection or account deletion✅ Immediate (never stored)
Written policy available publicly✅ This document
