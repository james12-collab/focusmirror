Parental Consent Agreement

Version 1.0 · Effective June 21 2026

This agreement is for parents and guardians of users under 18 years of age. Please read carefully before allowing your child to use FocusMirror.

About FocusMirror

FocusMirror is a study performance tracking application designed for students. It uses the front-facing camera on a smartphone to measure study focus indicators including eye blink rate and postural alignment. It generates a focus score and provides study recommendations.

FocusMirror is built by a student, for students, with privacy and wellbeing as core design principles.

What We Collect From Your Child

What We DO Collect

  • Username and hashed password (never plain text)
  • Numerical focus scores derived from camera analysis
  • Session duration and study timing data
  • Anonymized behavioral patterns (peak study hours, average scores)

What We DO NOT Collect

  • Video footage or images of your child's face
  • Raw biometric data
  • Real name, phone number, or email address
  • Location data (unless they choose to appear on the World Map)
  • School name or class information (unless they join a class session)

⚠ Camera processing happens entirely on your child's device. No video or images are ever sent to our servers. Only derived numbers (blink rate: 14, posture score: 87) are transmitted.

Safety Features for Minors

  • Midnight Lock: The app automatically stops tracking after midnight to protect sleep. One weekly override is available for legitimate late study sessions.
  • Suggestion framing: All recommendations say "This is a suggestion. You know yourself best." — never commands.
  • No social media integration: Sharing is optional and controlled by the student.
  • Parent Dashboard: You can view your child's daily focus level (High, Medium, or Low) without specific scores to avoid creating pressure.
  • No advertising: FocusMirror is completely ad-free. Your child will never see advertisements.

Parent Dashboard — What You Can See

If you choose to use the Parent Dashboard at focusmirror.onrender.com/parent:

  • ✅ General focus level: High, Medium, or Low
  • ✅ Number of study sessions today
  • ✅ Total study time today
  • ✅ Current study streak (days in a row)
  • ❌ Specific focus scores (hidden to prevent pressure)
  • ❌ Emotion data (private to student)
  • ❌ Real-time monitoring (daily summary only)

Your child knows exactly what you can see. There are no hidden data channels.

Your Rights as a Parent

  • You can request a copy of all data stored about your child
  • You can request deletion of your child's account and all associated data
  • You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us or deleting the account
  • You can object to AI training use of your child's data (opt-out available in account settings)

Data Sharing

We do not share your child's personal data with:

  • Advertisers
  • Third-party companies
  • Other students (scores are private unless child shares them)
  • Teachers (unless child joins a class session)
  • Anyone without explicit consent

Parental Consent

By allowing your child to create an account on FocusMirror, you confirm that you have read this agreement, understand how the Service works, and consent to your child's use of FocusMirror under the terms described above.

If you have questions before consenting, please contact us through the app.
