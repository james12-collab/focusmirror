🍪 FocusMirror uses minimal cookies and browser storage. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies. This policy explains exactly what we store and why.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They allow websites to remember information about your visit. FocusMirror uses both traditional cookies and browser localStorage to provide core functionality.

Managing Cookies

Browser Cookies

You can control and delete browser cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling the session cookie will require you to log in every time you visit.

LocalStorage

You can clear localStorage through your browser's developer tools (F12 → Application → Local Storage → Clear). This will reset your preferences and require re-onboarding.

Browser-Specific Instructions

Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Clear browsing data

Firefox: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data

Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data

EU Cookie Consent

For users in the European Union, we rely on the following legal bases for cookie usage:

Strictly necessary cookies: Legitimate interest — required to provide the Service you requested.

Functional localStorage: Legitimate interest — storing your preferences on your own device for your convenience.

Since we do not use non-essential tracking cookies, we do not display a cookie consent banner. If we add any optional cookies in the future, we will update this policy and implement a consent mechanism before doing so.
